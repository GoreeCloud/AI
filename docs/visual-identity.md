# GoreeCloud AI visual identity

## Status

The artwork in `public/artwork/` is a **candidate product identity** created during Milestone 0. It is stored in the authoritative application repository so it can be reviewed, iterated, versioned, and later approved without relying on temporary design-tool exports or external-only files.

It must not be represented as the final approved GoreeCloud AI identity until the GoreeCloud visual-identity review and approval requirements are satisfied.

## Concept

The primary symbol is an **intelligence aperture**: six outer nodes connected to a central luminous core. The concept represents local model intelligence, retrieval, tools, research, and orchestration converging through one GoreeCloud AI product boundary.

The mark intentionally avoids letter-only branding, generic chat bubbles, robot heads, and direct imitation of ChatGPT, Claude, Ollama, or another AI product. Its silhouette is intended to remain recognizable independently of color.

## Canonical assets

- `public/artwork/icon.svg` — scalable candidate application icon and primary product mark.
- `public/artwork/logo-lockup.svg` — horizontal candidate logo lockup for repository, documentation, website, and release surfaces.

The icon SVG is the preferred canonical source for future raster and platform derivatives. Web favicons, PWA icons, Linux launcher assets, Android adaptive assets, future iOS assets, GoreeCloud Manager entries, and release artwork must remain traceable to the approved canonical source.

## Design requirements

Future identity work must preserve:

- Glaze UI family coherence without making GoreeCloud AI interchangeable with other GoreeCloud applications.
- A distinct silhouette and concept rather than differentiation by color alone.
- Strong small-size recognition.
- Light- and dark-surface resilience.
- Cross-platform identity continuity.
- Repository-local authoritative source ownership.
- Accessible product naming alongside icon-only presentation where context requires it.

## Approval gate

Before this candidate becomes official, review must verify originality, Glaze UI conformance, practical rendering at required sizes, platform derivative requirements, contrast, recognizable silhouette, and consistent use across all initial release surfaces.
